Please review our dataset context and usage terms

⚠ Important — Model Limitations

This model was trained on 10,000 images out of the full 84,000-image MRL Eye Dataset. Results reflect this constrained training subset. Confidence scores are indicative only and should not be used for any clinical, medical, or safety-critical decision-making. Performance may vary across different eye types, lighting conditions, and camera qualities.

1. Purpose of This Application

VigilEye is a research prototype built to demonstrate the feasibility of transfer learning–based drowsiness detection for general-purpose use cases including student alertness monitoring, remote work fatigue detection, and occupational safety awareness.

2. No Medical or Safety Guarantee

This system does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. It must not be relied upon in safety-critical contexts including but not limited to driving, operating heavy machinery, or medical monitoring.

3. Data Privacy

Images you upload are sent to a local inference server only and are not stored, logged, or transmitted to any third party. No personally identifiable information is collected.

4. Model Accuracy Disclaimer

The MobileNetV2 model achieves 88.56% accuracy and 90.59% Recall on the MRL validation set under controlled conditions. Real-world performance may differ. The model was trained on a 10k subset; full dataset training is ongoing research.

5. Acceptable Use

This application is for educational and research demonstration purposes only. Commercial use, redistribution, or deployment in production systems requires explicit written permission.

6. Limitation of Liability

The developers of VigilEye accept no liability for any decisions made based on the output of this system.
